# I Like to dance West Coast Swing
___
[[Vault/Learning West Coast Swing is fun\|Learning West Coast Swing is fun]]. It is one of the best things I think I have ever done. It has introduced me to a whole host of new people and experiences that I would never trade for the world. 

In general, my hope is to host here my knowledge and insights on West Coast Swing including things like its history, my journey, and technical details and skills I've learned from comps, workshops, and more.
